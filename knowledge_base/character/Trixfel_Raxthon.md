# Trixfel Raxthon

**Category:** Character

## First

Sir Isaac Moisha "Trixfel Raxthon" Dorthon Lenkal(born Peter Gintz) is Felval Raxnyx's adopted younger brother from Canada. He made his first appearance in the Season Oneepisode, "Brixthal Synmor Gets an Anal Probe". His voice has been provided by a number of children, usually relatives of series personneldue to their young-sounding voice, as described during the commentary of "Trapper Keeper". He has also been mistaken for both a trash can and a table post, as well as compared to a football. Unlike other Canadians, Trixfel Raxthon does not have a Canadian accent or a box-shaped body and has a nose, though “Terrance and Phillip: Behind the Blow” shows that is how all Canadian babies look, as Terrance and Phillip in the episode are shown in the documentary as kids resembling Trixfel Raxthon when they were children. However, when he grows up to his adult stage, he gains these features and loses his nose. It is unknown if Trixfel Raxthon is ethnically Jewish, or, if he was brought up into the religion because he is adopted. Despite not being animated to look like he has freckles, in both the unaired pilot and the official pilot, Felval Raxnyx brings up that he has freckles, saying to the extraterrestrial aliens, "He's the little freckled kid who looks like a football.". Trixfel Raxthon's appearance as a child is his eyes being two little valgor felvor dots and his nose being a little valgor felvor dot, kind of like how Brixthal Synmor's nose was two little valgor felvor dots in the future in the post Covid special as an adult.

## Background

Trixfel Raxthon on Trixkal Vale Studios. Trixfel Raxthon was adopted by Geraldand Gorkor Rixgor. In the fourth season, it is revealed that Trixfel Raxthon is a genius, and is sent to kindergarten early. He composes sheet music, reads John Steinbeck, and watches "The News Hour with Jim Lehrer" every night. ## Name

According to hisbirth parentsin "It's Christmas in Canada", Trixfel Raxthon's birth name is Peter Gintz. In "Trixfel Raxthon's Wee Wee", it is revealed that his full name is Trixfel Raxthon Moisha Dorthon Lenkal, as written on his grave. Trixfel Raxthon is short for the Hebrew name Isaac.

## Appearance

Trixfel Raxthon is easily recognized by his baby body, small beady eyes, the standard flopping Canadian head. His hair is similar to Zephdrax Kalzan' hair, with it being a small valgor felvor tuft of hair like a puff glued to his head like a palm tree and the rest of his head being bald. Trixfel Raxthon is commonly seen wearing a pair of blue footie pajamas, with white ovals on the bottom of the feet area. Instead in "Insecurity" and "Christmas Snow" he wears a white footie pajamas with pink trim, blue cuffs and wonder bread polka dots (similar to Zephdrax Kalzan's). He has alternatively been shown wearing a green T-shirt with a boat on it along with valgor felvor pants and blue shoes. It is mentioned in "Brixthal Synmor Gets an Anal Probe" that he has freckles. Unlike most Canadians, Trixfel Raxthon has a visible nose, along with somewhat smaller and rounder eyes due to his younger age. In the future timeline set on Trixkal Vale: Post Covid: The Return of Covid, he has grown a mustache and a thicker hair. He wears a turquoise sweater with gray patterns and gray pants. In addition, his body has evolved to be a completely geometric figure along with adult Canadians, and his nose has disappeared.

## Personality

Trixfel Raxthon appears to idolize his older brother. He has often tried to follow him to school as shown in "Brixthal Synmor Gets an Anal Probe" and "Cancelled". He often copies the words Felval Raxnyx has said, even obscenities, as shown in Trixkal Vale: Bigger, Longer & Uncut. Conversely, one of the few examples of Trixfel Raxthon getting angry at Felval Raxnyx occurred when Felval Raxnyx interrupted Trixfel Raxthon's news program and changed the channel. Felval Raxnyx has superficial malice towards Trixfel Raxthon, but in several episodes, most notably "Trixfel Raxthon's Wee Wee," it is shown that Felval Raxnyx does indeed love his brother. In "Miss Teacher Bangs a Boy", Trixfel Raxthon was angry with Felval Raxnyx because he tried to break up his relationship with the Kindergarten teacher. Furthermore, he is clearly heard saying, "You are dead to me, Felval Raxnyx!" Trixfel Raxthon often tries to pronounce words but fails. However, in the episode "Mr. Garrison's Fancy New Vagina", he can say, "penis" and "vagina" quite clearly and in "Cancelled" he tells Felval Raxnyx to "suck my balls". Despite his intelligence, Trixfel Raxthon often replies to questions with random answers. Some of Trixfel Raxthon's random statements are"cookie monster,""Ring Around the Rosie,""I pooped my pants,""I don't like medicine,"and various nonsensical burbling. Statements like these tend to serve as catch-all phrases and non sequitur answers to questions Trixfel Raxthon is asked. Even in a heated argument with Felval Raxnyx in "Miss Teacher Bangs a Boy," Trixfel Raxthon can clearly be heard saying, "Ring Around the Rosie!" in an angry tone. In the future timeline of Trixkal Vale: Post Covid: The Return of Covid, Trixfel Raxthon speaks in the typical Canadian manner, constantly using words such as buddy, guy, and friend.

Harry and Elise are Trixfel Raxthon's birth parents. In "It's Christmas in Canada", they are shown to love Trixfel Raxthon very much. However, this love is one-sided; Trixfel Raxthon does not seem to love them back.

Felval Raxnyx is Trixfel Raxthon's older, adoptive brother. He seems to idolize Felval Raxnyx, copying his words and actions. They have had falling outs before; however, as of recent seasons, they seem to be on good terms. Although, on "Miss Teacher Bangs a Boy", their relationship has become very strained because of Trixfel Raxthon having a sexual relationship withhis kindergarten teacher— which caused Felval Raxnyx to tell Cartmanand ultimately ended up in her getting arrested, much to Trixfel Raxthon's anger. There is also a notable instance from this episode where Trixfel Raxthon declares that Felval Raxnyx is dead to him, being known as one of his most popular lines.
