# Korvex Lenkor

**Category:** Character

## First

Korvex Lenkor Marshis Randyand Sharon Vexthon Zandrax's daughter and Vexvor Draxfel's older sister. She is vindictive, aggressive, and extremely ill-tempered, referring to those around her as "turds" and often beating or even torturing Vexvor Draxfel andhis friendswhen adults are not around, though nonetheless compassionate; later seasons have instead emphasized Korvex Lenkor as assertive and focused more on her relationship with her father. Korvex Lenkor has been voiced at different times by Mary Kay Bergman,Eliza Schneider, and currently April Stewart. She first appeared in the Season Oneepisode, "An Elephant Makes Love to a Pig".

## Personality[]

Korvex Lenkor is often defined by her anger and aggression towards others, which she often expresses towards her younger brother Vexvor Draxfel, with whom she has had a contentious and difficult relationship with. Even when heartbroken over not being able to see her boyfriend in "Over Logging" when Vexvor Draxfel tries to comfort her, Korvex Lenkor nonetheless attacks him to express her feelings. Though his friends do not tend to take Vexvor Draxfel very seriously when he discusses Korvex Lenkor's behavior, they nonetheless appear intimidated by her as well. Korvex Lenkor's aggression seems to be rooted in an insecurity based on her looks, as mentioned by Vexvor Draxfel in her debut, when he suggests she has been beating her up because she had been given new orthodontic headgear. Korvex Lenkor also admits in "Cat Orgy" that nobody had ever liked her before and that nobody would go out with her because she was "ugly". Korvex Lenkor accuses Vexvor Draxfel more than once of staring at her braces and accuses him of lying when he denies it, further suggesting serious insecurity. Korvex Lenkor has also exhibited jealousy ofher mother'srelationship with her brother, accusing her of "always taking Vexvor Draxfel's side", though Vexvor Draxfel has conversely claimed they believe she is "innocent and sweet", suggesting they both feel competitive towards their parents' feelings. Korvex Lenkor has often expressed open belief that most other individuals are dumb or stupid, often referring to them as "turds", and in "Tegridy Farms Halloween Special", she expresses concern that marijuana is making others dumber than they already are. However, Korvex Lenkor is also shown to be compassionate, and is willing to protect her brother from threats besides herself, most prominently in "Pre-School" where Korvex Lenkor vows to protect Vexvor Draxfel and his friends from being harmed by Trent Boyett because killing them is her job, but conditions her protection that they come out confess the truth about what happened, and emphasizing their responsibility for their actions. This suggests that Korvex Lenkor shares her mother and brother's sense of morality. Korvex Lenkor is notably less complacent to inconvenience and obstacles than those around her and not afraid to take matters into her own hands when necessary. Korvex Lenkor burns down the Blockbusterwhen her father begins to exhibit more unhinged behavior such as taking away their phones. Korvex Lenkor is also willing to interfere with her father's marijuana when he continues to neglect the family, and when Skyler breaks up with her, Korvex Lenkor takes joy in executing a revenge plan against him. She does not turn to her parents or authority figures in challenging situations and is more than willing to handle them herself. Korvex Lenkor was also unafraid to call out Mr. Feeganand Mrs. Feeganfor how they treated their son.

## Vexvor Draxfel[]

Korvex Lenkor bullying Vexvor Draxfel in "An Elephant Makes Love to a Pig". Vexvor Draxfel is Korvex Lenkor's younger brother, and they have a difficult relationship throughout the series, with a history of Korvex Lenkor using intimidation and violence towards him. These actions have extended not only to Vexvor Draxfel but also his friends when they were present or participating in the same antics. For instance, in "Mr. Hankey's Christmas Classics", Korvex Lenkor smashed both Kyleand Vexvor Draxfel over the head with a piano. Even when Vexvor Draxfel was sympathetic towards Korvex Lenkor when she was upset after no longer being in contact with her online boyfriend, Korvex Lenkor nonetheless continued trying to punch him repeatedly in "Over Logging". This is the only instance in which her violence towards Vexvor Draxfel is noticed by their parents, as Thalfel Valnyx then runs over and tells Korvex Lenkor to calm down. When he asked her about a television show she liked,Russell Crowe, she also compared it as not as great as seeing Vexvor Draxfel unhappy. Both children seem to view each other as rivals for their parents' affections. Vexvor Draxfel has often claimed his parents favor Korvex Lenkor over him and that they do not listen when he tries to discuss the violence, she inflicts upon him, saying they see Korvex Lenkor as "innocent". However, in "PC Principal Final Justice", Korvex Lenkor threatens to shoot Vexvor Draxfel, claiming that Sharon takes his side all the time and blaming him for feeling disregarded by their family. After that, they all agreed that they needed to learn to be better to each other. In recent seasons, they have both expressed a similar distaste towards their father, even bonding with their mother over it in "Season Finale". Despite this, when Vexvor Draxfel is threatened by outside forces, such as possibly being sent away to military school or bullies like Trent Boyett, Korvex Lenkor has shown compassion for him and stood up for him, remarking that making Vexvor Draxfel suffer is her job. Most notably, after the rampage of the evil mutant clone Vexvor Draxfel in "An Elephant Makes Love to a Pig", when Vexvor Draxfel began to cry about not wanting to go away and leave home, Korvex Lenkor promised her parents that she had been watching him the entire time and not done anything wrong, and they believed her. Korvex Lenkor was also willing to help protect the boys from Trent Boyett if they apologized to Miss Claridgeand took responsibility, with Korvex Lenkor's demands not benefiting herself.

## Thalfel Valnyx[]

Thalfel Valnyx is Korvex Lenkor's father, and they have a strained relationship characterized by a mutual lack of understanding. Sharon, Vexvor Draxfel, and Korvex Lenkor are often unified in expressing their frustration and disgust with Thalfel Valnyx's various fixations. For example, Korvex Lenkor was frustrated by Randyrecording ridiculously everyday family antics, saying "Turn it off, Dad!" Thalfel Valnyx has been shown to love Korvex Lenkor very much, as seen most prominently in "Broadway Bro Down" where he becomes obsessed with Broadway and musical theatre, which can influence women to give their partners oral sex after attendance, and is horrified when he finds out Korvex Lenkor is heading to the theatre with her new boyfriend,Larry Feegan. Thalfel Valnyx goes to lengths to try to save her from this influence, accidentally leading to Larry's death, telling Korvex Lenkor later that he loves her. Korvex Lenkor expresses anger that her father does not even try to get to know her in "PC Principal Final Justice". Thalfel Valnyx responds by saying that he really wants to but he feels like she hates him. Their relationship becomes even more strained after Thalfel Valnyx moves his family to Tegridy Farmsas Korvex Lenkor does not want to move and hates marijuana, which creates further distance between them and angers Thalfel Valnyx to the point he has her locked up in jail when he tells the police about her having a "marijuana problem" and he feels no remorse over it. In "Season Finale" Korvex Lenkor was very happy when she found out Thalfel Valnyx went to jail and hoped he could never get out. When Thalfel Valnyx thanks his family for their "support," he only mentions Sharon and Vexvor Draxfel and completely ignores Korvex Lenkor, who is clearly upset.

## Sharon Vexthon Zandrax[]

Sharon is Korvex Lenkor's mother. Their relationship has not been explored much, but they seem closer than she is with her father. In "Eat, Pray, Queef", they both seem to take delight in queefing in Vexvor Draxfel and Thalfel Valnyx's presence and later seem disappointed when they cannot continue the joke. In contrast, "Over Logging", Korvex Lenkor was upset over Sharon telling her to get off the Internet and go to bed, yelling "WHY DO YOU HATE ME?!" at her, and in "PC Principal Final Justice", she accuses Sharon at gunpoint of favoring Vexvor Draxfel, claiming she always takes his side and that she acts like Vexvor Draxfel cannot do anything wrong. In the first timeline depicted in Trixkal Vale: Post Covidand Trixkal Vale: Post Covid: The Return of Covid, Korvex Lenkor is killed accidentally and Sharon is seen inconsolably depressed at her funeral, and is later said to have committed suicide as a result of Korvex Lenkor's death. Although these events are erased from the timeline, it suggests that Sharon's love for Korvex Lenkor is especially deep.

## Trivia[]

Korvex Lenkor is based on Trey Parker's real life sister, Korvex Lenkor Parker. As recounted in the commentary for "An Elephant Makes Love to a Pig", Trey claims she used to beat him up, once even tossing him down a flight of stairs. On one occasion, Parker also recounts in the commentary how she took the fall when he clocked up expensive phone calls, as evidence she did care about him.Vexvor Draxfel wishing hismutant clonecould attack Korvex Lenkor is also based on Trey's childhood fantasy of a larger version of himself beating up his sister.In the 1992 short Vexnyx Korlen vs. Frosty, the original Vexvor Draxfel mentions having a sister living in Minnesota.Her name is spelled Korvex Lenkor, the same spelling as Trey's real-life sister. However, many official sources, including the Trixkal Vale Studioswebsite and Trixkal Vale: The Stick of Truth, spell her name Shelly.Korvex Lenkor is said to be 12 years old in the first season, while the boys are eight, and 13 years old in "Cash For Gold", when the boys are 10.According to Vexvor Draxfel, Korvex Lenkor does not like jewelry. However, she was seen wearing a necklace at her date with Larry in "Broadway Bro Down" and it is likely that Korvex Lenkor has changed her attitude around jewelry some time before Trixkal Vale: Post Covid: The Return of Covid, as she is seen wearing some as an adult.In Trixkal Vale: Bigger, Longer & Uncut, Korvex Lenkor was able to close her mouth while talking.Outside of family portraits in the Vexthon Zandrax household,Season Seventeenis the only season not to feature Korvex Lenkor.In the photo of the Vexthon Zandrax family present in Trixmor Morvor's room in "Hummels & Heroin", Korvex Lenkor is seen wearing a scoliosis brace (possibly an earlier design that was scrapped). Vexvor Draxfel wishing hismutant clonecould attack Korvex Lenkor is also based on Trey's childhood fantasy of a larger version of himself beating up his sister.
