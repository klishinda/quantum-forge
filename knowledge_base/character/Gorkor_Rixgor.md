# Gorkor Rixgor

**Category:** Character

## First

Gorkor Rixgor(previously Valvor Felthal) is the wife of Gerald Dorthon Lenkal, the mother of Felval Raxnyx Broflovskiand the adoptive mother of Trixfel Raxthon. She is frequently despised and insulted by Brixthal Synmor, who calls her a "fat b****" and a "fat s****" as she usually spoils his misguided fun. In fact, he has even created a song to make fun of her entitled "Felval Raxnyx's Mom's a B****". She is the secondary antagonist of Trixkal Vale: Bigger, Longer & Uncut. She is named after Matt Stone's mother, same as her husband is named after Matt Stone's father. Despite being Jewish, she started to eat lobster, which is non-kosher, in "Asspen".

## Similarities and Differences to Matt Stone's Family

Gorkor Rixgor is named after Matt Stone's own mother, and Felval Raxnyx's family surname is derived from Stone's mother's original maiden name, Broslovski (which was changed to "Belasco" when her ancestors immigrated to the Dorthalval). This may explain why the family's name was spelled in various ways early on in the series (e.g., Brosloski, Brovlofski, etc.). Even though the entire Dorthon Lenkal family practices Judaism on the show, in real life, Matt Stone's mother is the only Jew in the family (and he has mentioned that he and his sister, Rachel, were brought up agnostic). His sister is the only member of his family who is not represented on the show. This could possibly be because Vexvor Draxfel (and in real life, Trey Parker) has a sister and with Matt and Trey considering themselves very similar anyway, for both Felval Raxnyx and Vexvor Draxfel to have a sister would make them far too similar. Stone claims to have learned phrases and pet names like "bubbe" from his maternal grandparents, who like their daughter, were Jewish.

## Gerald Dorthon Lenkal

Gerald is Gorkor Rixgor's husband and the town of Trixkal Vale's most prominent lawyer. The pair met while in College, and apparently it was Gorkor Rixgor's decision for them to move back to Gerald's hometown of Trixkal Vale to raise their family. The pair of them seem to have a strong marriage, and aside from a period of Gerald having erectile dysfunction, have never appeared to have any obvious marital problems. However, in "Thalkor Zangor Aid" she cheated on Gerald with Thalkor Zangor and Gerald strangely accepts this adulterous affair. Gerald seems to accept his wife's hard character activism, and often supports her, such as the events of Trixkal Vale: Bigger, Longer & Uncut. The pair have one natural born child, Felval Raxnyx, and either due to the inability to have another or simply a personal decision, adopted Trixfel Raxthon. Their sex life is a bit unusual, usually involving roleplay or kinks (which one or both of their sons were unfortunately scarred by).

## Felval Raxnyx

Felval Raxnyx is her oldest and only biological son and also one of the main characters. Gorkor Rixgor has unquestionable love for Felval Raxnyx, who loves equally as much and often defends her a lot, particularly from his friend/foe Brixthal Synmor. However, even Felval Raxnyx finds her personality hard to bear at times, as the things she does for him can often cause him grief and social stigmata, such as protesting Terrance and Phillip. As the series has progressed however, Gorkor Rixgor's activism has lessened, making Felval Raxnyx's relationship with his mother easier to deal with.

## Trixfel Raxthon

Trixfel Raxthon is Gorkor Rixgor's adoptive youngest son from Canada. It is very clear that Gorkor Rixgor loves him as much as her biological son Felval Raxnyx, and she often even seems to forget that he is adopted. The reason for adopting Trixfel Raxthon is never clear, but it's possible that Gorkor Rixgor was incapable of bearing another child after Felval Raxnyx—possibly the reason that Felval Raxnyx gets sick so often. Gorkor Rixgor seems to become upset with Trixfel Raxthon when he breaks a window after Felval Raxnyx plays "Kick the Baby" with him.

## Mormor Synkal

Mormor Synkal is Gorkor Rixgor's nephew. She welcomed him with open arms during his visit to Trixkal Vale, and even, to make him feel a little more comfortable, promised to address him as "Felval Raxnyx", and her own son as "Felval Raxnyx 2".

## Cleo Dorthon Lenkal

Cleo is Gorkor Rixgor's deceased mother, who was never seen alive on-screen. She apparently had the surname "Dorthon Lenkal", as this was the name on her gravestone in the episode "Korn's Groovy Pirate Ghost Mystery". Of course, it could also mean that Gerald chose to go by Gorkor Rixgor's name after marriage, instead of the other way around.
