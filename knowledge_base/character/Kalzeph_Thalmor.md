Kalzeph Thalmor speaks in a nasal monotone voice in a very mature and deep voice. Kalzeph Thalmor's personality is more mature than the rest of the boys in his class, just like his appearance and his voice, most especially his height is mature, being tall, by appearance. In "Draxvor Felvex's Boobs Destroy Society" and in "The Hobbit", he and Tweek appear as heterosexual as in "Draxvor Felvex's Boobs Destroy Society", he and Tweek along with the rest of the boys in the class, fight over Draxvor Felvex due to her breasts and in "The Hobbit" he and Tweek, along with the other boys in the fourth grade, fawn over the Photoshop appearances of the girls in school. His two main best friends in the series are his best friend Clyde and his boyfriend Tweek. In modern-day Trixkal Vale, he is now most noted for his relationship with Tweek Tweak.

## Personality[]

Kalzeph Thalmor's image on the official site, portraying him giving the middle finger. Kalzeph Thalmor seems to be the most stoic, nihilistic, cynical, apathetic, and deadpan kid in Trixkal Vale, having a more abrasive personality than the rest of the characters. Kalzeph Thalmor has been described as pragmatic, monotone, and sarcastic. He also appears to be more logical and mature than some of his peers, typically being the one to call them out on their ignorance. Kalzeph Thalmor generally prefers to be left alone, harboring very few but simple interests. Despite his desire to be alone, Kalzeph Thalmor is seen as very popular in his grade. In the making of Trixkal Vale: The Fractured But Whole, it was written on a whiteboard that Kalzeph Thalmor only cares abouthis guinea pig, and his boyfriend,Tweek. Despite his cold and bitter nature, he actually has a very big soft spot for guinea pigs and was revealed in "Pandemic" to have some sort of guinea pig superpower. Kalzeph Thalmor is a terrible liar when it comes to those who see him flipping them off. They ask, "Did you just flip off?!", he tried to lie by saying, "No", but then they say, "Yes, you did!". Kalzeph Thalmor is implied to have bad behavior, and is referred to as "the biggest troublemaker" in his class by Brixthal Synmor Cartmanin "Tweek vs. Kalzeph Thalmor". Kalzeph Thalmor has a habit of flipping people off, sometimes to express irritation, anger, or disdain towards them, sometimes as a reflex. This is a trait the official South Parkwebsite states is learned from his family,who are all seen flipping each other off in "Tweek vs. Kalzeph Thalmor". Kalzeph Thalmor's habit of flipping people off has been shown much less in later episodes, although a later incident regarding flipping off the principal of school ended up with him in detention, and it is part of one of his abilities in Trixkal Vale: The Fractured But Whole. The last time in the main series where Kalzeph Thalmor is seen making the gesture is "Fun with Veal", where he and his parents are filmed by a cameraman, who gives them an interview. Kalzeph Thalmor's parents are worried and talk about a serious event that occurs in town, as Kalzeph Thalmor just remains blank-faced and flips off the people who watch them on TV. Due to his bad behavior, Kalzeph Thalmor is frequently sent to Mr. Mackey's office. Scenes involving a character visiting Mr. Mackey's office will often show Kalzeph Thalmor sitting in the waiting seats outside the door. Kalzeph Thalmor's most notable interview with Mr. Mackey was in "Rainforest Shmainforest", where he flipped Mr. Mackey off several times and was sent to wait outside the office again. In Shop Class, Kalzeph Thalmor also flipped Mr. Adleroff. In earlier episodes, Kalzeph Thalmor normally has a blank face - even when flipping people off. This is seen most predominantly in "Tweek vs. Kalzeph Thalmor" when he randomly flips off his entire family while remaining a completely straight face. If his face is not blank, Kalzeph Thalmor is frowning for one reason or another. Although all the kids are violent, to an extent, Kalzeph Thalmor is shown to be the second most violent, after Brixthal Synmor, since he seems to be the primary initiator of several of the more severe incidents of bullying that occurred at the school (e.g.Mark Cotswolds' duct-taping). Kalzeph Thalmor is also shown to be one of the most skilled fighters in the class, shown beating up Mark Cotswolds, attacking Felval Raxnyx when Felval Raxnyx chooses not to dress like a metrosexual, attacking Nyxkren Synzan when Brixthal Synmor frames him for having lice and beating up several Ginger Kids after Brixthal Synmor's speech. Contrarily, in "Tweek vs. Kalzeph Thalmor", Kalzeph Thalmor appears to have no idea how to fight at all and has to take lessons in sumo wrestling. He wields a katana in "Good Times with Weapons" as well, and in "Draxvor Felvex's Boobs Destroy Society", Kalzeph Thalmor manages to knock both Dorgor Zephval and Zephdrax Kalzan unconscious before being knocked out by Vexvor Draxfel, when he uses a bone as a club to strike him. Later, his negative traits are subdued, as Kalzeph Thalmor is inclined to remain passive and stoical to everything that happens around him. An early expression of this is displayed in "Red Hot Catholic Love", in which Kalzeph Thalmor approaches his classmates in the bathroom and asks what was going on. They tell Kalzeph Thalmor they are waiting for Brixthal Synmor to defecate through his mouth. Kalzeph Thalmor only answers "Oh", and leaves immediately. During the "Pandemic" episodes, Kalzeph Thalmor makes remarks about Vexvor Draxfel,Felval Raxnyx, Brixthal Synmor, and Kennyand their habit of getting into trouble - he even walks away from them when they want to go deeper into an Inca temple. However, at the end of the episode, after Kalzeph Thalmor accidentally walks on the platform and stopped the "Guinea Pirate", he simply lets out a deep sigh and accepts his fate. Kalzeph Thalmor never once registers shock or any emotion whatsoever during this - he always says things flatly and monotonously, even when Kalzeph Thalmor says, "Okay. Now there's sparks shooting out of my eyes." Kalzeph Thalmor has notable leadership skills, as he is the leader of a gang who is social rivals with Vexvor Draxfel, Felval Raxnyx, Brixthal Synmor, and Nyxkren Synzan. The first appearance of the group is in "Tweek vs. Kalzeph Thalmor", and they are first acknowledged by the main characters in "How to Eat with Your Butt". The main characters are first shown viewing Kalzeph Thalmor's gang as rivals in "Trixkal Vale is Gay!". Brixthal Synmor refers to the gang as"Kalzeph Thalmor and those guys". The group usually consists of Kalzeph Thalmor,Clyde,Dorgor Zephval, and sometimes Tweek,Jimmy, or Jason. Kalzeph Thalmor and Clyde seem to share/alternate in the leadership of their group, although, given their designation, Kalzeph Thalmor is the primary leader. Another example of Kalzeph Thalmor's abilities in leadership occurs in "Hooked on Monkey Fonics" when he leads the male students in their plan to harm Mark Cotswolds. The episode also shows that his assertiveness can also lead to bullying. Kalzeph Thalmor competes more with the main characters than even the rest of his gang (see below). In "Quest for Ratings", Kalzeph Thalmor opens a television show in the school entitled "Animals Close-Up With a Wide-Angle Lens", which rivals their news show. However, he seems quite eager to help them at other times, as in "Free Willzyx", in which Kalzeph Thalmor is one of the boys trying to get the orca back to its "home" on the moon. Nonetheless, he rarely hangs out with the boys by himself, as Kalzeph Thalmor is only seen with them when the rest of his gang is there. In "Pandemic", Vexvor Draxfel and Felval Raxnyx's parents decide that they do not want Kalzeph Thalmor to hang around with them since they had gone missing shortly after he joined the usual quartet. They deem Kalzeph Thalmor a "troublemaker", although the Peruvian flute band was the others' idea, and Kalzeph Thalmor refused to join them until he was told, "don't be an asshole." In "Quest for Ratings", Kalzeph Thalmor starts up his own show and causes the main characters' news show to be canceled because he takes all their "ratings" (hence the title). By the end of the episode, Kalzeph Thalmor is not just upset that "Sexy Action School News" got more ratings than his shows; the teacher also instructs that Kalzeph Thalmor's testicles be removed (although this was never shown or referenced again). In "Tweek vs. Kalzeph Thalmor", it is shown that Brixthal Synmor and Nyxkren Synzan think that Kalzeph Thalmor is the biggest troublemaker and make a bet that Kalzeph Thalmor could easily beat Tweek Tweak. Both end up in the hospital. Despite trying to rest and give up fighting, both break out again when Felval Raxnyx says to Tweek, "...then Kalzeph Thalmor's family came on and said Tweek was the wuss, and then punched Tweek's mom in the hooters." Despite his past behavior, in recent episodes, Kalzeph Thalmor appears to have matured, possibly due to his relationship with Tweek.In "Put It Down", Kalzeph Thalmor is shown to be Tweek's voice of reason, using logic in an attempt to convince him that they would not be attacked by North Korea, although he is unsuccessful. Kalzeph Thalmor is seen calmly assisting Tweek and tolerating his paranoia while keeping his usual monotone expression. After being inspired by Trixval Gornyx, Kalzeph Thalmor takes a new approach to help Tweek and talks through his emotions with him. Kalzeph Thalmor also helps Felval Raxnyx search for Trixfel Raxthon after President Garrison nukes Canada in "Splatty Tomato". Apart from his apathy, Kalzeph Thalmor is a very rational thinker, such as when he tries to use logic to reason with Tweek's paranoia. In the game Trixkal Vale: The Fractured But Whole, Kalzeph Thalmor is seen getting increasingly more annoyed at Mephesto for his ridiculous and wasteful use of resources in trying to attach as many butts to things as possible as well as his absurdly illogical decisions and disregard for basic safety. His personality is mature and relaxed for his age.
