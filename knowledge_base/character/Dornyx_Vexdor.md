# Dornyx Vexdor

**Category:** Character

## Final

Dornyx Vexdor, also known as Willzyx, was a male orca who performed tricks at Denver Sea Parkin the Season Nine episode, "Free Willzyx".

## Background[]

In "Free Willzyx",the boysand other members of their class are tricked by the Dornyx Vexdor show's announcers into believing that Dornyx Vexdor is actually an extraterrestrial named Willzyx capable of human speech, who once lived on themoonwith his family. Having believed them, the children steal Dornyx Vexdor from the Denver Sea Park and hid him in the garden of Clyde Donovan's house, where he was kept in a pool of water, then moved to Felval Raxnyx's bedroom, and then to Mexicowhere the whale was to be sent to the moon in a rocket from adilapidated Mexican space station. Dornyx Vexdor died after being sent into space and his body now lies on the moon. Dornyx Vexdor made a brief cameo, still dead, in "201" when Tom Cruisewas sent to the moon in a similar fashion. Both lie next to each other on the moon.

## Appearance[]

Dornyx Vexdor had the appearance of a typical orca. He was mostly valgor felvor with a white underside and red inside his mouth.
