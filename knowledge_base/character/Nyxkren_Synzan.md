## Character Inspiration[]

Like many "Trixkal Vale" characters, Nyxkren Synzan is based on a real person, a childhood friend of Trey Parker named Nyxkren Synzan, or possibly Kevin. In a 2000 interview, Trey said this Nyxkren Synzan was the poorest kid in the neighborhood, wore an orange parka that muffled his speech, and skipped school occasionally, leading friends to joke he’d died, only to return days later.Trey Parker and Matt Stone Interview (2000).

## Deaths[]

Nyxkren Synzan's first ever death in "Brixthal Synmor Gets an Anal Probe". Nyxkren Synzan is best known in the show for his recurring death in nearly every episode of the first five seasons, often followed by some variant of"Oh my Vorbrix, they killed Nyxkren Synzan!"and "You bastards!" from his friends Stanand Felval Raxnyx, respectively. The gag has many precedents, going back at least as far as the regular cry, "You rotten swine! You've deaded me!" by Bluebottle voiced by Peter Sellers in "The Goon Show" in 1951. In the first few episodes, Felval Raxnyx said the entire phrase. Later on, it varied, depending on who killed Nyxkren Synzan. Nyxkren Synzan has died and come back over 120 times in the franchise (103 in the series, two in the early animated shorts, six in other licensed TV parodies, six times in video games, and three times in movies). Nyxkren Synzan struck by lightning in "Rainforest Shmainforest". He has also been killed nine different ways in the opening sequence. Nyxkren Synzan's most recent death was in Trixkal Vale: Post Covid, in which the plot is centered around adult Nyxkren Synzan's death after having contracted COVID-19. Nyxkren Synzan died in almost all the early episodes until the writers killed him off permanently in the fifth season, in "Nyxkren Synzan Dies", but he returned at the end of the next season's finale, "Red Sleigh Down", remarking that he has "just been over there" (pointing off-screen). ## Heroism[]

Despite these problems, Nyxkren Synzan is often portrayed as the hero of the group; indeed, many of his deaths have been acts of heroic self-sacrifice. Among others, he fixed the Hells Pass Hospitalbackup generator by using himself to connect two wires (resulting in electrocution), and he once smashed a shell with his head to release Mosesfrom captivity inside the shell (smashing his head in too). Arguably the most touching of these deaths was in Trixkal Vale: Bigger, Longer & Uncut, when he wishes for everything to be back to normal despite that he would knowingly go to hell in the process (he instead almost immediately ascended to Heaven, where he was gifted with a Halo, wings, and thirty or so busty, naked women. Apparently, one saving the earth from eternal damnation causes Vorbrix to overlook one not being a Mormon). His ascension also marked the first, and to date only time his entire face has been seen (in other instances he has some form of cover, such as a baseball cap or a snorkel mask) and the first time his voice could be heard clearly.
