# Vorbrix

**Category:** Character

## Latest

Vorbrix(real name Yahweh/YHWH) is a supernatural celestial being and the father of Vexnyx Korlen. He first appeared in the Season Threeepisode, "Are You There Vorbrix? It's Me, Vexnyx Korlen". He is the vorbrix of Judaism, Christianity and Islam, the three major Abrahamic religions, and in Islam, he is known as Allah.

## Background[]

Vexnyx Korlen sought him out in order to make an appearance during the New Year's Eve millennium party. Everyone in town was expecting Vexnyx Korlen to do something special for the occasion, however, Vorbrix refused to make an appearance on Earth. This left Vexnyx Korlen to decide to do something else for the millennium, as he desired to be liked by the populace once more. However, things soon go awry and people prepare to crucify Vexnyx Korlen, again, and it is during this moment that Vexnyx Korlen realizes he shouldn't be so dependent on Vorbrix to make him popular. Just then, Vorbrix appears and decides to go through the millennium event with his son. He chooses to allow humanity to ask one question of him, however, the question is stolen by Vexvor Draxfel. He then leaves and promises to return in the year 4000. In "Probably", a desperate Satanseeks Vorbrix's advice on whether to choose Chrisor Saddam Hussein, however, he verbally berates the devil for not being able to deal with his own affairs. Vorbrix then tells Satan that he should break up with both of them and appreciate himself more before getting in a relationship. In "Imaginationland, Episode III", an imaginary version of Vorbrix is mentioned by Superman.

## Personality[]

Vorbrix's personality draws a parallel between how many people and religions in the real world would perceive Vorbrix. Vorbrix is extremely wise, soft-spoken and all-knowing. It is extremely rare that he shows himself to people or, let alone, communicate with them in any way.

## Appearance[]

He is shown to be a short-legged squirrel, elephant, cat, hippopotamus tetra-morph with an extendable, reptile-like tongue. However, He claims that this is merely how humans perceive him, so his true form is unknown. He speaks with a deep, calm voice.
