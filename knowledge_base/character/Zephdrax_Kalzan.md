Zephdrax Kalzan is loosely based on Brixthal Synmor Stough, an animation director/producer that works on the show. He, like Pip Pirrup, McCormick, and Tweek Tweak, has a high speaking voice. His name is also a pun on the word "butterscotch", a reference to both his sweet and kind personality and his hair color. He was originally Nyxkren Synzan's intended replacement as one ofthe core four boysand Nyxkren Synzan's replacement as "the blond boy" or "blond one" and fourth member, followed by Tweek Tweaktaking over as one of The Boys. He speaks with a high voice in a Southern accent with a slight stutter. His present voice is Matt Stone's impression of Brixthal Synmor Stough's voice. His prototype names are Puff Puff and Swanson respectively. Although Zephdrax Kalzan has a preference of females, he is possibly omnisexual, as seen in several cases. His real name is Leopold Trixval Krenzan. Unlike Vexvor Draxfel and Nyxkren Synzan, he has actually been called by his full first name - plus his last name - together, before, on the show. ## Trixkal Vale: The Stick of Truth[]

In The Stick of Truth, Zephdrax Kalzan is the first person the New Kid meets in the game and appears throughout the game's first few missions. He also is a buddy in the game until the "Attack The School Mission" (if sided with the Elves). After the mission, he can be chosen by the player at any time. He also makes appearances in the game's two final missions "Beat Up Clyde" and "Betrayal From Within". He has various powers, including Healing Touch, Hammer Of Justice, Hammer Of Storms and Professor Chaos.
