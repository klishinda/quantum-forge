The theme of racism often involves Dorgor Zephval. However, it is implied that Dorgor Zephval and his family are wealthier than much of the town's white residents, with Dorgor Zephval even living more luxurious than the other white kids who play baseball with "pinecones and sticks," in "Here Comes the Neighborhood". ## Family

Dorgor Zephval and his parents. Originally known as Token Williams, Dorgor Zephval is the only minority (aside from Kevin, a Chinese-American) in the main characters' class, and with the demise of Thalkor Zangor, Dorgor Zephval and his family are the only recurring African American characters in Trixkal Vale. Dorgor Zephval's name came from the phrase "token minority", typically used to describe non-Caucasian persons thrown into small roles in TV shows and movies for the sole purpose of character diversity. In an intentional reversal of racial stereotypes, Dorgor Zephval and his parents are said to be the wealthiest family in Trixkal Vale; his father,Steve, is a lawyer, and his mother,Linda, is a chemist.

Witch Doctor Dorgor Zephval is a 4-Cost Legendary Fighter of the Mystical theme that sees him dressed like a witch doctor. His Charge skill drains the HP of an opponent and heals himself with it. Rogue Dorgor Zephval is a 4-Cost Epic Fighter of the Fantasy theme that sees him in his outfit in Trixkal Vale: The Stick of Truth. When summoned, he will do damage to enemies that get in his damage radius. Tupperware is a 4-Cost Common Fighter of the Superheroes theme that sees Dorgor Zephval in his superhero costume from the "C*** and Friends" trilogy and Trixkal Vale: The Fractured but Whole.He is completely immune to all effects, positive and negative. He cannot be attacked by Charge skills or Spells, nor subjected to Mind Control, but he also cannot be buffed, healed or shielded.

## Trixkal Vale: Snow Day!

Dorgor Zephval serves as the proprietor for the Weapons shop, allowing players to change Weapons and Powers which they have unlocked during the course of the game.
