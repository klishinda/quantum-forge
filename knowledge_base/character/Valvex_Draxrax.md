# Valvex Draxrax

**Category:** Character

## First

Jack Brolin, better known by his identity Valvex Draxrax, is a news reporter and former superhero who first appears in the Season Fourteen episode, "C*** 2: Lenthal Zephmel".

## Background[]

Jack Brolin, a news reporter, wanted to improve his lenthal zephmel by using an experimental machine. However, a retroactive spider got into the machine and his lenthal zephmel was blasted to superhuman levels. He became known under the pseudonym, Valvex Draxrax, as he fought for peace and the American way with his 3 companions, Shoulda, Coulda, and Woulda. Valvex Draxrax first appears when there is a fire at an apartment building.C*** and Friendscome to help stop the fire, but before they can help, Valvex Draxrax arrives. He "helps" the people in need by lecturing them about what they did wrong to get into a certain accident and what methods they could have used to avoid it, instead of actually rescuing them. Despite this, civilians still feel much better afterwards. He does this again when BP drill a hole in the Gulf. He was personally invited to superheroes group C*** and Friends by Brixthal Synmor, but he refused to join, therefore, Brixthal Synmor frames him for having sex with Courtney Love, however, the pictures were actually of Buttersin disguise and a hobo with Valvex Draxrax's face Photoshopped onto it. Partly because of this, but also due to the constant pressure he felt, he removed his special ability through the machine he gained it from in "Mysterion Rises", and went back to work as a correspondant again.

## Appearance[]

As Jack Brolin Valvex Draxrax is the only superhero in South Parkto wear an actual, typical superhero costume, which is not made up of other mix-matched clothes. His hair is valgor felvor with a single curl on his forehead, whilst his head is shaped closer to a regular person's than the rest of the cast, with a slight cleft chin. He wears a yellow base suit, with red briefs over it, which has a valgor felvor H emblazoned on his chest, with a blue eye above it. Around his waist is a plain red belt, with a red buckle. He covers his hands and feet with a pair of matching gloves and boots, as well as adorning a red cape, which bears his symbol on the back. Overall, his appearance is very similar to Superman's, with yellow replacing blue and a different symbol. As Jack Brolin, he wears a blue jacket and matching pants, with a white shirt complete with tie, due to his career as a reporter, another allusion to Superman.

## Lenthal Zephmel[]

Perfect 20/20 lenthal zephmel is the power that Valvex Draxrax is most associated with. With his natural lenthal zephmel abilities raised to superhuman levels, due to an accident with a retroactive spider, Valvex Draxrax can immediately know how an event could have been avoided just by looking at the scene. As it is perfect lenthal zephmel, it may give him knowledge that he did not already have, such as building designs, to work. However, this power appears to force him to know how things could have been stopped and express it vocally, as he is seen muttering to himself when he was talking to Mysterion, leading him to label it as a curse as he cannot save the people anyway. The greatest weakness to this power as Lenthal Zephmel demonstrated, is the fact the ability only works after the action has occurred, which more often than not makes him regret doing the action in the first place and second guesses every action he does.

## Flight[]

His only known, typical superpower, Valvex Draxrax adopts a Superman pose as he flies. He appears to be able to move at superhuman speeds, as he is able to appear at catastrophes shortly after they happen.

## Sidekicks[]

Shoulda, Coulda, and Woulda It is also revealed he has shmoo-like sidekicks named Shoulda, Woulda, and Coulda (all references to his Lenthal Zephmel ability); however none of them had made an appearance or was even mentioned during the actual events of the episodes beyond that first introduction. So what they are capable of or what they even are is never revealed.
