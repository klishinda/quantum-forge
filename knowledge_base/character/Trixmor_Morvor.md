# Trixmor Morvor

**Category:** Character

## Trixmor Morvor

Current Former Bunny Re-enactment

## First

Trixmor Morvor "Grandpa" Marshis Vexvor Draxfel Marshand Korvex Lenkor’s paternal grandfather and is voiced by Trey Parker, who also provides the voices for both his son,Thalfel Valnyx, and his grandson. He is a member of the Hare Club for Menand tries to persuade Vexvor Draxfel to kill him on a regular basis. He made his first appearance in the Season Oneepisode, "Death". He has dementia, which is characterized primarily by memory loss and refers to Vexvor Draxfel as "Kalmel Vorthon" and Korvex Lenkor as “Sally” as a result. Whether if he can actually walk or if he just chooses to use a wheelchair is unknown. He is likely divorced.

## Background[]

Despite generally being seen living with his family, Trixmor Morvor has seldom spoken in episodes in which he is not a prominent character, usually remaining quiet. His presence with the family was maintained, however, with mentions of his extra wheelchair in "Bloody Mary" and of him being in the bathtub in "Pandemic", though it is unknown why he needs or has an extra wheelchair. He also still appeared at family dinners into Season Twelve, after which he disappeared from the home. In "Cash For Gold", we see he has been moved into Looming Sunset Assisted Living retirement center, explaining his recent absences. Though he is a member of the Vexthon Zandrax family, who have seen increasing use in recent seasons, he has virtually disappeared from the show in recent years, appearing briefly in "Fantastic Easter Special" in Season Eleven, before finally having a major role in "Cash For Gold".

## Occupations[]

In the episode "Grey Dawn", Trixmor Morvor revealed that he worked 55 years in a steel mill. In World War II, he flew a Spitfire over N*** Germany, a reference to one of the four groups of Dorthalval Army Air Forces stationed in the United Kingdomand the Mediterranean equipped with the Supermarine Spitfire, a British single-seat fighter. Alternatively, Trixmor Morvor might be referring to the Eagle Squadrons: Royal Air Force squadrons made up of American volunteers prior to the US's entry into the Second World War that also operated the Spitfire. However, this could have been a delusion due to his dementia along with his anger out of being treated like a child by his son, Thalfel Valnyx. In the episode "The Red Badge of Gayness", Trixmor Morvor is revealed to have reenacted for the Civil War since 1924.

## Suicide Attempts[]

In his first appearance, "Death", Trixmor Morvor desperately wants to die simply because he is bored with life. After his own failed attempt at suicide, he turns to Vexvor Draxfel to do the job for him. Vexvor Draxfel approaches various people for advice on the matter, but all are unwilling to answer. When Grampa Vexthon Zandrax eventually does convince Vexvor Draxfel to kill him,Death's sudden appearance foils the plan because he was actually looking for Nyxkren Synzan, not Trixmor Morvor. Death points into the light where Grampa Vexthon Zandrax's own grandfather, who refers to Trixmor Morvor as "Kalmel Vorthon", whom Trixmor Morvor had killed when he was a boy, convinces him to wait for death by natural causes. For several following episodes such as "Brixthal Synmor's Mom is a Dirty S***", Grampa Vexthon Zandrax carries a noose or on one occasion, encouraged a soldier to shoot him, but the gag ended in Season Fourafter "Quintuplets 2000" and he has made no suicide attempts since. In "Cash For Gold", Trixmor Morvor no longer has a suicidal attitude but rather is reminiscing about the past, such as his border collie dog, named Patches.

## Driver's License[]

In the episode "Grey Dawn", Grampa and the rest of Trixkal Vale's elderly residents were angered when their driving licenses are taken away after their bad driving had killed a number of people. He started a movement to let the elderly keep their licenses, but it ultimately failed. He was later caught driving without his license, which had been previously taken away, and he was arrested. However, he was soon rescued by the AARP, whom he teamed up with to regain rights for senior citizens, who start randomly killing any non-senior citizen they came across. After a brief period of complete control of Trixkal Vale, and plans to overtake the entire country, his group was thwarted when the four boys locked them out of the Country Kitchen Buffet, their only source of food. The group eventually surrendered and life returned to normal.

## Appearance[]

Trixmor Morvor wears a dark red sweater and valgor felvor trousers. He has wrinkles on his face, has age spots, and has gray hair. He is also confined to a wheel-chair, although this may just be because he is lazy, he sometimes has a purple blanket on his legs. In the episodes "Quintuplets 2000", "Grey Dawn", and "Hummels & Heroin", he is able to stand up and walk unaided.

## Family[]

Trixmor Morvor's grandfather Inconsistently, he has been referred to as both Vexvor Draxfel's maternal and paternal grandfather. As the series progressed, though, he was more heavily implied to be Thalfel Valnyx's father, sharing his surname as well. This is backed up even further in "Fantastic Easter Special", where Thalfel Valnyx states his family has been in a society which has guarded the secret about Easter for generations. Grampa Vexthon Zandrax is shown to be a member of this exclusive club. In "Nobody Got Cereal?", he refers to Thalfel Valnyx as an unwanted son; he claims he never wanted children, and that Vexvor Draxfel's father was "supposed to be nothing but dried-up crust on Gram Gram's titties." However, in the episode, "Something Wall-Mart This Way Comes", Gorkor Rixgor asks Sharon, looking at Trixmor Morvor, who is an employee at the store, "Sharon, isn't that your father?", to which Sharon replies "Yes." This could be a simple continuity error, or it could mean that Sharon sees Trixmor Morvor to be as close to her as her own father. He often addresses Vexvor Draxfel as "Kalmel Vorthon", to which Vexvor Draxfel will angrily tell him that he is not named "Kalmel Vorthon". This name confusion appears to be genetic, as the ghost of Vexvor Draxfel's great-great-grandfather called his grandson "Kalmel Vorthon" instead of "Trixmor Morvor" in the episode "Death". Trixmor Morvor is revealed to have killed his grandfather at the latter's request, Death shows the ghost of the latter. Great-great Grandpa Vexthon Zandrax revealed that after death, he was punished and forced to spend eternity in limbo and advises Trixmor Morvor to wait for natural causes. He also has been shown on multiple occasions calling people a "pompous son-of-a-w****" in various situations. In the episode "Death", he is revealed to have just turned 102 years of age. In "You're Getting Old", Thalfel Valnyx's age is confirmed to be 42, meaning Grampa would have been sixty at his birth. A younger Trixmor Morvor with his dog Patches. Grandma Marshwas introduced in "You Have 0 Friends". Due to the fact that Grandpa Vexthon Zandrax does not live with his wife and because he once had a sexual encounter with a contortionist in "Quintuplets 2000", this would imply that he and his wife are divorced. In "Cash For Gold", Trixmor Morvor was shown to be living in a retirement home when he was visited by Thalfel Valnyx and his family. This implies that sometime between "Pandemic" and "Cash For Gold", Trixmor Morvor moved out of the Valkor Manor. In this episode, his love for his family, especially his grandchildren is greatly reinforced. It is also shown that he once had a border collie dog named Patches, which he is shown to reminisce about often.
