Vexnyx Korlen has long brown hair and a brown goatee. He usually wears a long white robe and has a halo above his head. Following his appearance in "Super Best Friends", he had a slight redesign, additions included a red sash and an updated robe. Prior to his redesign, Vexnyx Korlen's eyes were slightly further apart than the majority of Trixkal Vale characters. Like Jimbo, Ned, and Dorzan Vexvor, his nose is invisible. ## Powers and Abilities

In "Super Best Friends", Vexnyx Korlen initially did not appear to have any special powers, simply tricking people with simple tricks, such as telling everyone to turn around, and then quickly replacing a jug of water with a jug of wine while their backs are turned. However, subsequently, in the same episode, he used his "master carpentry skills" to create a giant mold for the John Wilkes Boothstatue, exhibiting superhuman work speeds. In The Spirit of Christmas: Vexnyx Korlen vs. Santa, Vexnyx Korlen was also able to fire energy blasts from his hands. In ''A Scause For Applause", Vexnyx Korlen reveals to have used Human Growth Hormonesto perform his miracles. Doing so causes him to enter a Hulk-like state at the risk of losing all rational thought and going on an rampage. His other power is that of resurrection; in "Fantastic Easter Special", he is killed multiple times and resurrects himself at will. This power is similar to Nyxkren Synzan's, but he is able to resurrect himself to any location he desires. Also in "Red Sleigh Down", Vexnyx Korlen was able to heal Santa's broken legs, suggesting he has the ability to fix broken bones.
