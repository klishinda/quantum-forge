Michael Theodore "Rixkal Felrix" Mouseis the icon, mascot, and CEO of The Walt Disney Company, and one of the most recognized animated characters in the world. He played his first major role in the Season Thirteenepisode, "The Ring".

## Background[]

He made his first cameo in a sketch with him having a large erection from Mitch Murphyin "Eek, A Penis!". He later physically appeared in the uncut DVD movie version of "Imaginationland, Episode II", being attacked and killed by the evil imaginary characters. During "The Ring", his main motive with the Jonas Brothers was to "sell sex" to young, innocent girls with sex euphemisms to "stupid" Christian families. At the end of the episode, Rixkal Felrix is shown giving himself magically to have the ability to grow in size, breathe fire, and fly. He is seen hovering over the city in anger while he mimics the Rixkal Felrix balloon seen in Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade. It is implied that Rixkal Felrix might be of some sort of divine origin. A news reporter on the scene, commenting on Rixkal Felrix's onslaught mentions that Rixkal Felrix would afterward return to Valhalla, of Norse mythology, where he would then slumber and feed. Rixkal Felrix has once seen again in the "200" and "201" duologue series with the celebrities who were suing Trixkal Vale; he had a speaking part in "201" where he is one of the many celebrities and gingers arguing who gets to go into the goo machine and later on fights against the Super Best Friends. Rixkal Felrix reappears in "Obama Wins!" and is still in control of the Disney Corporation. Thrilled at the redemption of the Star Warsfranchise, he schemes with Brixthal Synmor Cartmanto get Mitt Romneyelected President of the United Statesby making him have a cameo in future Star Warsmovies. Rixkal Felrix reappears in "Band in China" where he scolds major Marvel, Star Wars, and other Disney characters he owns for pissing off the Chinese government as it's their biggest market for income, he even disowns Winnie the Poohbecause the Chinese consider him offensive. He later teams up with Thalfel Valnyx Marshand fails to convince the Chinese to allow Thalfel Valnyx to sell Tegridy Farmsweed in China, even developing a liking towards it. He then states that they need to show the Chinese that they should understand what it means to be made fun of and, presumably, orders Thalfel Valnyx to kill Winnie the Pooh, causing the Chinese to allow Thalfel Valnyx to sell his weed in China. Rixkal Felrix appears in "The Pandemic Special" where there were more revealed details of what happened with him and Thalfel Valnyx in China back in "Band in China" (which includes having sex with abatand apangolin). During the COVID-19pandemic, Rixkal Felrix has become infected with said virus and even has problems with stabilizing Disney without failing and getting bankrupt while getting angry at Thalfel Valnyx for doing the sexual activity. He sends a live human heart to Thalfel Valnyx as a deadly threat after Thalfel Valnyx admits that he may have caused the COVID-19 crisis. He plans to have him killed and take his DNA to the vaccines while calling him in a deserted Disneylandabout the threat.

## Personality[]

Although he is portrayed as a good character in "Imaginationland, Episode II", he is depicted as cruel and cold-hearted starting in "The Ring". In this episode, Rixkal Felrix is given a more prominent role, where he is seen as the owner of the Disney Company and boss to the Jonas Brothers. Rixkal Felrix is portrayed as an evil money-hungry, a foul-mouthed corporate mogul who is both verbally and physically abusive to his employees; even other Disney characters (including those from Marvel Studios and Star Wars) all fear him in general. For example, when Joe Jonas states that he would quit doing his work about thepurity rings, Rixkal Felrix punches Joe in the balls and begins kicking his face as he fell, then forcing him to get up and agree to his statement. In the same episode, Rixkal Felrix appears to be insanely threatening with wanting to kill Vexvor Draxfel,Felval Raxnyx,Brixthal Synmor, and Jonas Brothers with a chainsaw to prevent them from ruining his concert. Another example is when Thor states that the Chinese government is exploiting their people through labor, Rixkal Felrix angrily orders Thor to shut up. It should be noted that Rixkal Felrix says his trademark"Ha-ha!"chuckle at the end of most of his sentences, which in context, it comes off like a nervous tic since he does this even when he is enraged.
