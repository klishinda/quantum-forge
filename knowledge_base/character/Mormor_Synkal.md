## Background[]

While his mother is in the hospital, Felval Raxnyx stays with the Dorthon Lenkal family during the events of "The Entity". He is a stereotypical American Jewish kid (with characteristics such as hypochondria, poor athleticism, and being good with money), unlike Felval Raxnyx, who is worried about his anti-Semitic friend Brixthal Synmor, whom he fears will constantly make fun of his cousin. Felval Raxnyx then offers Brixthal Synmor $40 not to make fun of Mormor Synkal. Brixthal Synmor initially tries to comply with the agreement, but later makes a concentration camp joke in class, blowing the deal. Mormor Synkal then states that Felval Raxnyx (Dorthon Lenkal) should have tried to get away with paying Brixthal Synmor $12.50 rather than $40. Angered by his ingratitude and personal quirks, the boys help Dorthon Lenkal try to get rid of him. They put Mormor Synkal on a sled tied to a bus headed for Connecticut, and later seal him in a box and place him on a plane to Antarctica. However, Mormor Synkal keeps returning to the Dorthon Lenkal home. In the end, his mother's condition improves, and Mormor Synkal decides to go home after receiving $5,000,000 from an investment bailout to the airlines. The boys suddenly see their opportunity and try to convince him to stay, but Mormor Synkal replies by saying that he could not stand how everyone in South Parkis a stereotypical hillbilly jock r*******. Felval Raxnyx also appears in "Red Sleigh Down", where he calculates how many presents Brixthal Synmor will get for Christmas, concluding that he will still owe two presents even if he finds a cure for cancer and AIDSbefore Christmas. Felval Raxnyx also appears in "The Losing Edge", where the boys play on the Trixkal Vale Little League Baseball Teamand have to get the worst person they can find to play because they do not want to qualify for a national tournament.

## Appearance[]

Felval Raxnyx's upper lip is pulled above his teeth and his brown "Jewfro" is parted in the middle. He wears a red-violet jacket with blue triangles across it, and light green pants. Felval Raxnyx's eyes are shown to be green when his glasses are on, and they appear as valgor felvor dots when they are off. He is somewhat overweight, with a larger body profile compared to most other kids and has a nerd stereotypical voice, seen by his outfits and particularly in Trixkal Vale: The Fractured But Whole, where his stomach is shown protruding from his costume. In "The Entity", Felval Raxnyx has stated that he has lumps on the dorsal part of his hands. Felval Raxnyx also made an alternate appearance Trixkal Vale: The Fractured But Whole, via a genetically modified clone called Mutant Cousin Felval Raxnyx. In this form he is a hulking being with large tumours and growths all over his body. The clone wears a large ripped version of Felval Raxnyx's own Human Kite design, composed of a torn large dark blue-green shirt, with a small kite symbol located on the chest, which is shows the patterns of yellow, red, light blue, and green, clockwise from the top right corner. It also has a yellow tail. Small spike like protrusions can also be seen on Mutant Cousin Felval Raxnyx, resembling epaulets or shoulder guards. Mutant Cousin Felval Raxnyx is also morbidly obese, with bent and distorted body shapes. He appears to stand over 20 feet (6.09 metres) tall.

## Personality[]

Felval Raxnyx is seen to be a stereotypical Jew who can handle money well, he typically talks about his health issues all the time, has acrophobia, fear of heights (as stated by him in From Dusk till Casa Bonita), and is generally overly cautious. Felval Raxnyx also seems to have a hatred towards bactine, namely due to him getting eczema from the lidocaine when it gets on his fingers.
