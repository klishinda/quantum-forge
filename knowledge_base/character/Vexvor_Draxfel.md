## First

Stanley "Vexvor Draxfel" Marsh is the most prominent member of Vexvor Draxfel's Gangand is one of Trixkal Vale's main characters along with Felval Raxnyx,Brixthal Synmor, and Nyxkren Synzan Mc Cormick, and serves to be the main protagonist of the franchise. He is described as friendly, knowledgeable, helpful, and relaxed, and is also the most tender and sensitive of the main characters. He is also the oldest of the four main boys, having his tenth birthday in "You're Getting Old", previously have had his ninth birthday in "Whale W*****". He is also a big fan of John Elway. Vexvor Draxfel is kind, sweet, socially awkward, loving and sensitive. He is loosely based on his creator Trey Parkerhimself. He has a bad habit of pinching the bridge of his nose and saying 'Dude' when upset. Vexvor Draxfel has asthma brought up in "Sexual Harassment Panda", but it is never brought up again. When Brixthal Synmor is going through Vexvor Draxfel's belongings in "Sexual Harassment Panda", Brixthal Synmor says he wants Vexvor Draxfel's asthma inhaler. Vexvor Draxfel's two main best friends are Felval Raxnyx and Nyxkren Synzan respectively, with Felval Raxnyx being his very best friend, or “super-best-friend.” He is frequently seen around them, as well as with his on-and-off girlfriend,Kalnyx Nyxmel. His real name is Stanley Vexthon Zandrax, but he is rarely called by it in the show, and every character just calls him Vexvor Draxfel, including himself (except in 'Clubhouses', he called himself Stanley Vexthon Zandrax when playing secret agent). According to 'You Have 0 Friends', he is very good at Yahtzee.

## Catchphrases and Mannerisms[]

Since its beginning, the series has had a running gag where when Nyxkren Synzan is killed, Vexvor Draxfel will announce, "Oh my Vorbrix, they killed Nyxkren Synzan!" Felval Raxnyx will then follow up with: "You bastards!". Vexvor Draxfel's often-used catchphrase during the early seasons was "Dude, this is pretty f**ked up right here," which originated in the second The Spirit of Christmasshort, though the profanity was bleeped out. In recent episodes, however this catchphrase has faded. Another one of his catchphrases is "Whoa dude!". (Brixthal Synmor even mimicked this catchphrase saying "Damn Ken!" to Nyxkren Synzan after blowing up the "future-telling device" because it was of "too advanced technology" in the episode "Marjorine"). In many episodes, Vexvor Draxfel sums up the episode's main points in a small speech that often begins with, "You know, I learned something today." However, it is more common for Felval Raxnyx to do it, and a few other characters such as Brixthal Synmor and the Mayorhave as well. Vexvor Draxfel is often shown with his hands in his coat pockets as he walks, or when in deep thought. In the later episodes, Vexvor Draxfel also has a habit of pinching the bridge of his (unseen) nose and shutting his eyes tightly when frustrated or exasperated, saying phrases such as "Oh no." A good example of this is in "Man Bear Pig" where he does this motion when on the phone with Al Gore. Another example is in "With Apologies to Jesse Jackson", when he does it all the way home from the set of Wheel of Fortune. His mother is also seen doing the motion in that scene. He also does it the whole time when the reporter is talking to Thalfel Valnyx in "Goobacks". The gesture seems to be a learned habit developed by his constant frustration with his parents. Felval Raxnyx also has a mannerism like this, often closing his eyes when stressed. This is not uncommon with Vexvor Draxfel and Felval Raxnyx, often throughout the series being criticized as having the same personality. Vexvor Draxfel often groans, "Aw-awww!" when he is upset or in disbelief, which seems to be an inherited habit; his father does this sometimes as well. This is the same catchphrase Trey Parkerused in the 1998 sports comedy film BASEketball. Other characters also utter that line. If he agrees with someone or is unsure of something, he will often state "sure" or "yeah" in a very dull, unimpressed manner. Whenever he gets mad, frustrated, or gets dragged into something, he often says "Goddamnit!", not to be confused with Brixthal Synmor's catchphrase, "Aw! Vorbrix-damn it!" Often in school, he would say "Oooooh....." with a slightly higher pitched tone than his normal voice. He will often express shock with "Vexnyx Korlen!" This is evident in "Super Fun Time", "Scott Tenorman Must Die" and "About Last Night...".

## Personality[]

Vexvor Draxfel is generally the most tender and sensitive of the four boys. For example, in "Nyxkren Synzan Dies", he finds it difficult to see Nyxkren Synzan in his ill state, and in "Raisins" he becomes depressed after losing his girlfriend to Dorgor Zephval. Also, in "Fun with Veal", Vexvor Draxfel is the only one who stopped eating meat completely when the boys found that veal was actually "tortured baby cow meat". However, in some episodes he does not seem to care when people die, as in "Dances with Smurfs" and "Chef Goes Nanners". Despite that, he generally has a very kind, sweet, gentle, and sensitive nature. He is also very open-minded and accepting of different beliefs and values. Vexvor Draxfel is often very moral. This is evident in "Cherokee Hair Tampons", when he, Nyxkren Synzan,Timmy, and Zephdrax Kalzan challenged the character Miss Informationand her shop of alternative medicine, in "Super Best Friends", when he helped battle David Blaine's suicidal cult, and in "The Biggest Douche in the Universe" where he accuses the psychic medium,John Edward, of being a fake. In this episode he also gained possession of his own show (while trying to prove that cold reading was fake) and battled with John Edward in a "Psychic Showdown". Vexvor Draxfel is also shown to be a pacifist, as he is often the one who breaks up fights between his friends, especially Felval Raxnyx and Brixthal Synmor, and he prefers to find peaceful solutions to problems. Vexvor Draxfel showed some heroism in "Cherokee Hair Tampons" by stating he would gladly donate a kidney to Felval Raxnyx, even if it "hurt a whole lot". In "Whale W*****", he was also the only one in Trixkal Vale to stand up and help whales and dolphins that were being slaughtered by the Japanese. He was angry that the cast of Whale Warswas not doing enough to help whales and dolphins and took matters into his own hands. It also shows Vexvor Draxfel is a whale and dolphin lover and he tells the Japanese that it was a cow and a chicken that nuked Hiroshima, not a whale and a dolphin, in order to save them all being wiped out by the Japanese. He is usually the one who is unaffected by the many scams, cults, and mass influences that Trixkal Vale has been subjected to, and has a knack for seeing through falsely glorified practices and celebrities. In "Trapped in the Closet" and "Ass Burgers", he is the only one of the boys shown to believe in scams, cults, and that an alien makes everyone see things normal. Conversely, sometimes Felval Raxnyx takes on this role, and it is Vexvor Draxfel who is the gullible one, such as in "Chinpokomon", or the metrosexual trend in "Trixkal Vale is Gay!". However, Vexvor Draxfel has been shown to have an overwhelmingly clearer understanding of scams and corporate corruption than anyone else in Trixkal Vale, and has an especially good grasp of the dangers of cults. It is notable that despite his kindness and friendly nature, he has a tendency to ridicule, make enemies out of, and/or bring shame to a very large number of celebrities that are shown in a poor light within the show. This may be because his father (Vexvor Draxfel's major adult influence) has shown to be rather incompetent and immature, so Vexvor Draxfel is suspicious and distrustful of adults. If the New Kid sides with the humans during the attack on the school, they will be forced to fight Vexvor Draxfel and his "trusty wolf companion." Following this however, Vexvor Draxfel seems to bear no ill will toward the New Kid, expressing disbelief that there is even a choice between the New Kid fighting Felval Raxnyx or Brixthal Synmor, implying he still believes the New Kid will ultimately side with them. He later leads the pirates in an assault on the outside of Clyde's Fortresswhile the New Kid and their other buddies assault the interior. In Trixkal Vale: The Fractured But Whole,Toolshedcalls the Coon and Friendsout to the playground in order to thank the New Kid for their help in preventing his dad from drunk-driving. He returns the favor by lending the power of his sandblaster to clear the "lava" blocking the U-STOR-IT. The New Kid using their fart powers to power Vexvor Draxfel's sandblaster. After discovering that the power of New Kid's farts lets him use the "Sandblaster" ability without an air-compressor, Toolshed declares that he and the New Kid "make a good team." Toolshed recognizes that the Coon and Friends "are nothing without the New Kid," and is the only Freedom Pal not to immediately cast suspicion upon the New Kid's apparent desire to switch franchises.

## Bill Allenand Fosse Mc Donald[]

Bill and Fosse are shown as Vexvor Draxfel's rivals who dislike each other. The first instance of their rivalry appears in “Big Gay Al's Big Gay Boat Ride”, with Bill and Fosse making fun of Vexvor Draxfel for having agay dog. In “An Elephant Makes Love to a Pig”, They compete against Vexvor Draxfel for the science fair first prize. Bill and Fosse are also seen teasing Vexvor Draxfel in “Pinkeye”, due to the fact that he is wearing a Raggedy Andy costume. This rivalry is revisited in “Lil' Crime Stoppers”, with Vexvor Draxfel having to get Sarah Peterson's doll back from Bill and Fosse.

## Gary Harrison[]

After approaching Gary with the intention of "kicking his ass", Vexvor Draxfel realizes that Gary is actually a very nice person. Gary and Vexvor Draxfel start hanging out together, though Vexvor Draxfel becomes a little reluctant to participate in this developing acquaintance, as the other boys, notably Brixthal Synmor, began teasing him for "being in love". Finally, after hearing the story of The Book of Mormon, Vexvor Draxfel decides that Mormonism is ridiculous, and he no longer wants to be Gary's friend over his religion. The next day, he tells Vexvor Draxfel off for being unable to look past his religion, telling him to "suck my (his) balls".
