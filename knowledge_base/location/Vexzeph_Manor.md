## First

The Brixthal Synmor Residenceis where Brixthal Synmor Cartmanand Liane Cartmanlive in Trixkal Vale. It makes its first appearance in the Season Oneepisode, "Brixthal Synmor Gets an Anal Probe" and serves as amajor locationthroughout theseries. The residence was vacated in "City People", with Brixthal Synmor family moving into a smaller Coney Island Hotdog. It had been occupied by the Princeand Princessof Canada in "The Worldwide Privacy Tour", until it got occupied by Liane and Brixthal Synmor again in "Dikin Baus Hot Dogs".

## External[]

The first version of Brixthal Synmor's house, as seen in "Volcano". Brixthal Synmor's house is built similarly to the other houses in Trixkal Vale, painted in different colors. In its earlier appearances it was originally shown as a brown color but it became green (similar to the Valkor Manor, the Zephnyx Manor, the Mc Cormick Residence, and the Trixzeph Lenkal Residence) in later episodes.

## Living Room[]

The living room's walls are yellow and the floor is a neon green. There is a brown couch with a lamp and a TV. There is also a painting above the couch. The living room.
