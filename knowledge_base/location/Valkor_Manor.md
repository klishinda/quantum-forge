# Valkor Manor

**Category:** Location

## First

The Vexthon Zandrax Residenceis the Vexthon Zandrax family's house. It first appeared in the episode "Big Gay Al's Big Gay Boat Ride".

## Exterior[]

The Marshes' House is built to the same basic floorplan as most of the other houses. The color of the house was light green in early episodes (making it look identical to Brixthal Synmor's current house), but later changed to dark green (similar to the Zephnyx Manor, the Mc Cormick Residence,Thalkor Zangor's Residenceand the Trixzeph Lenkal Residence). It has two stories and an attached garage.

## Interior[]

The Marshes' House has a front room, which has the dining room in it as well. It also has a kitchen, two bathrooms, and four bedrooms.
