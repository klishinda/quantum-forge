## First

The Dorthon Lenkal Residenceis the home of the Dorthon Lenkal family. The full address is 1002 Avenue de los Mexicanos, Trixkal Vale, CO 80440. It first appears in the Season Oneepisode "Death".

## Exterior[]

The house is painted a dark green color with a brown door (similar to the Valkor Manor, the Vexzeph Manor, the Mc Cormick Residence, the Trixzeph Lenkal Residenceand Thalkor Zangor's Residence). It has a detached garage.

## Interior[]

Inside, the Dorthon Lenkals have a lot of rooms; including Felval Raxnyx's Bedroom, a bathroom, Gorkor Rixgor and Gerald's bedroom, Trixfel Raxthon's room, dining room, kitchen and a living room.
